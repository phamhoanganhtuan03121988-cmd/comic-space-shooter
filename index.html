HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • Drag anywhere on the screen — your ship copies the movement, so your finger never hides it.
  • Your ship fires automatically.
  • Tap NOVA (bottom right) to blast every enemy and erase bullets. It recharges.
  • Only the glowing core in the middle of your ship can be hit.
  • Desktop: WASD / Arrows to move, Space = NOVA, P / Esc = pause.

Power-ups

Critters

Combo & score

  • Chain kills quickly: 5 = x2, 10 = x3, 20 = x4, 30 = x5 score. Getting hit resets it.
  • Kill every enemy of a wave for a PERFECT WAVE bonus; take no damage for a NO DAMAGE bonus.
  • Spend coins on permanent upgrades between sectors.
